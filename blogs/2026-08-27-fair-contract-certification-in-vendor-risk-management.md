Vendor risk programs excel at measuring cybersecurity posture, financial stability, and regulatory compliance. But there is one critical layer most of them miss entirely: whether your vendor's contract terms are actually fair. That gap creates real problems for legal and procurement teams who approve agreements based on incomplete risk signals.
